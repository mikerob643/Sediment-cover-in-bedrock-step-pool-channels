# Data tables for “Sediment cover in bedrock step-pool channels counter-intuitively increases with increasing water discharge”, version 3

The tables of the paper by Albarran Ayala, A. J., Robinson, M. J., Takase, M., Inoue, T., & Scheingross, J. S., “Sediment cover in bedrock step-pool channels counter-intuitively increases with increasing water discharge”, Journal of Geophysical Research: Earth Surface, manuscript 2025JF008956R (in revision).

## What this version holds

`Tables_Combined_R2_2026-10-31.xlsx` (923544 bytes, md5 570b0aca325e623c137d135fe849bd2b): every table of the paper and of its Supporting Information, one
sheet per table, as revised for the second round of review (October 2026). It replaces `SI tables.xlsx` of version 2
(June 2026), which held the round 1 tables. In each sheet, the first row gives the table's title, the second row gives
the columns with their units, and the notes under the table define its symbols.

Setup Experiments formed the stepped beds (Low Slope, High Slope and Supplemental). Main Experiments ran three water
discharges over each stepped bed: L.1 to L.3 (Low Slope), H.1 to H.3 (High Slope) and S.1 to S.3 (Supplemental). The
methods are in Sections 2 and 3 of the paper and in the Supporting Information.

## Changes since version 2 (the round 1 tables)

- Table S1: the run time of the High Slope Setup Experiment, sediment supplies, measured transport capacities and supply-to-capacity ratios, flume tilt and measured initial slope, the planar-bed hydraulics recomputed with the right discharges, and the final slope of the Supplemental Experiment.
- Tables S2 to S4: the profiles recomputed with the right discharges and measured slopes.
- Table S14: its flow columns had repeated the Low Slope rows of Table 1.
- Tables 1 and S14: all values from one script, the analysis zone at the same position in every experiment of a slope, and a new column for the length of step faces and pools with subcritical flow.
- Tables 2, 3 and S15: a new column for the ratio of step height to step length, and a negative pool depth, meaning no pool, written as 0.
- Tables S5 to S13: the cover kept to the patches digitized from the photographs. The round 1 tables had carried cover past them, which gave negative flow depths in Tables S11 and S12 and impossible velocities and Froude numbers near the outlet in Tables S9, S11 and S12.
- Table S15: the thickness of cover normal to the bed, as in Table 2.
- Table S16: the column that gives the base cell size of the mesh headed as such.
- Table S17: new.
- Header and note wording, and the formatting of every sheet.

The elevation data of version 1 (October 2025) are superseded by Tables S5 to S13.
